THE RECOMMENDATION

Three markets. Three focused moves.

Our proposal protects the lending book while pursuing sustainable customer relationships.

GERMANY

GROW SELECTIVELY

Explore lending growth among customers with strong individual credit profiles. Germany has the highest average credit score and higher account balances.

FRANCE

STRENGTHEN THE BASE

Build on our largest customer market through financial education, savings support and suitable product offers.

SPAIN

BUILD EARLY RELATIONSHIPS

Explore university banking programs, starting with accounts and financial education. Offer credit only where affordability supports it.

OUR COMMITMENT Adjust lending rates responsibly as central bank rates rise, verify repayment capacity, and retain customers who can support long-term profitability. Before execution, check actual loan exposure, defaults and customer profitability.
